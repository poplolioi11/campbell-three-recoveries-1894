# 長期住院後復原的三個病例（1894） / On Three Cases of Recovery after a Lengthened Duration of Insanity, with Remarks — Transcription Dataset

J. A. Campbell 在 1894 年記述英格蘭卡萊爾郡立精神病院的三名長期住院者，追溯收治經過、院內活動、家屬接觸及後續離院情形。文章也比較作者早年報告的病例與其後追蹤。

J. A. Campbell’s April 1894 article recounts three long-stay patients at the Counties Asylum in Carlisle. The reports follow their reasons for admission, activities and contacts in hospital, and later leave or discharge. The article distinguishes these cases from those reported in 1888.

## Source and scope

College of Physicians of Philadelphia, Historical Medical Library, American Journal of Insanity 50(4), pp. 505–511, Source frames 1–7. Original publication: The American Journal of Insanity 50(4), April 1894, pp. 505–511. Complete April 1894 article, pp. 505–511, with its original notes and headings. This later article reports three new cases and cites the separate 1888 article of the same title.

## Files

- `transcription.txt` retains line and frame markers. Tabs mark table cells. 
- `transcription.json` identifies each frame and text or table unit. A null printed page means no printed page number was recorded. The source links identify the corresponding scan.
- `transcription.tei.xml` maps the same frame text and table cells into TEI P5.
- `review-notes.md` lists review coverage and reading decisions.
## Transcription and review

AI-assisted reading used fixed source scans. Published text preserves printed spellings and historical terminology. Spacing may be normalized. A square (□) indicates an unresolved character. See the notes for editorial omissions and uncertain readings.

Version 0.2.0. This dataset has no measured error rate or expert proofreading.
